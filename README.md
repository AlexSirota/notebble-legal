# Notebble — support and legal

Public pages that go with the **Notebble** app.

- [Support](https://alexsirota.github.io/notebble-legal/) — the App Store "Support URL" (this site's root)
- [Privacy Policy](https://alexsirota.github.io/notebble-legal/privacy_policy.html)
- [Terms of Use](https://alexsirota.github.io/notebble-legal/terms_of_use.html)

The Privacy Policy and Terms of Use are also bundled inside the app and can be
opened offline from Settings.

The support email address is hard-coded in `index.html` in four places (three
`mailto:` links and the visible address). Search for
`notebble-support@codeandsprites.online` to change it.

Notebble is an offline notes and reminders app for iOS and Android. It has no
account, no backend, and no analytics — everything you write stays on your device.
