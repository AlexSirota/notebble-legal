Privacy Policy

Notebble  ·  Effective date: September 21, 2026  ·  Version 1.1

Summary. Notebble does not require, request, or collect any personal data from you. There is no account, no registration, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no tracking. Everything you write in the app is stored only on your device. Any personal information you choose to type into the app is entered at your own discretion and at your own risk, and the Developer accepts no responsibility or liability for it.

1. Who we are and what this policy covers

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile application Notebble (the "App") handles information. The App is published by its developer and owner (the "Developer", "we", "us", or "our"). This policy applies to the App on iOS and Android. By installing or using the App you acknowledge that you have read and understood this policy. If you do not agree with it, do not use the App and uninstall it.

2. We do not collect personal data

The App is designed to work entirely offline and does not require any form of personal data from you. In particular, the App:

  • does not ask you to create an account or to sign in;
  • does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or any other identifier;
  • does not collect usage analytics, crash reports, or diagnostics automatically;
  • does not display advertising and does not use advertising identifiers;
  • does not track you across apps or websites;
  • does not transmit your notes, reminders, attachments, or settings to the Developer or to any server operated by the Developer.

The Developer operates no backend service for the App and has no technical means to access the content you create in it.

3. Content you create is stored on your device only

Notes, freeform text, reminders, categories, attachment references, and app settings are stored in a local database inside the App's private storage area on your device. This data never leaves your device through the App, except where you yourself export, share, or send it using other software on your device.

The App stores this content in unencrypted (plaintext) form using the standard file protection provided by your device's operating system. The App does not apply any encryption of its own. Anyone who gains access to your unlocked device, to a device backup, or to your device's file system may be able to read your content. You are solely responsible for securing your device (for example with a passcode or biometric lock) and for any backups you create.

Uninstalling the App permanently deletes all content stored by the App on that device. The App offers no cloud sync, export, import, or backup feature, and the Developer cannot recover deleted or lost content.

4. Personal data you choose to enter

The App does not require personal data, but it is a free-text tool and you may decide to type personal or sensitive information into a note, a reminder, or the freeform page, or to attach a file or image that contains such information. If you do so:

  • you do so entirely at your own discretion and at your own risk;
  • you remain the sole controller and owner of that information;
  • the Developer never receives, sees, processes, or stores that information;
  • the Developer accepts no responsibility and no liability for the loss, disclosure, corruption, or misuse of any such information, however caused.

We recommend that you do not store passwords, financial details, health data, or other highly sensitive information in the App.

5. Device permissions

The App may ask your operating system for the following permissions. Each is optional and is used only for the purpose described. Denying a permission does not affect your privacy; it only disables the related feature.

  • Notifications — to deliver the reminders you schedule as local notifications. Reminders are scheduled on the device itself; no push-notification server is involved.
  • Exact alarms (Android) — so reminders can fire at the exact time you chose rather than being delayed by the system.
  • Photo library / media / files — only when you tap "Image" or "File" to attach something to a note. The App stores a reference to the file you picked; it does not scan, upload, or index your library.
  • Camera — declared for attaching photos to notes. Camera access is only ever triggered by an explicit action of yours.
  • Run at boot (Android) — to re-register your reminders with the system after the device restarts.

6. Third-party services

The App itself makes no network requests to the Developer. The only network activity is limited to the following, each of which is governed by the privacy policy of the respective third party, not by this policy:

  • App Store / Google Play billing and RevenueCat. If you choose to purchase the optional Pro subscription, the purchase is processed by Apple or Google through their in-app purchase systems, and subscription entitlement status is managed by RevenueCat (Purchases SDK). These services may process transaction identifiers and an anonymous app-user identifier in order to validate and restore purchases. The Developer does not receive your name, payment details, or billing address. See Apple's, Google's, and RevenueCat's privacy policies for details.
  • Links you open. If you tap a link inside a note, the subscription-management page, or the "Report a problem" action, the App hands the link to your browser or store app. What happens after that is governed by those applications and websites.

The Developer does not control these third parties and is not responsible for their data practices.

7. Reporting a problem

The "Report a problem" action in Settings → About does not collect or send anything. It simply opens the App's public listing in the Apple App Store or Google Play (on iOS, directly at the "Write a Review" step) so that you can leave a review or comment there. The App does not gather any technical details about your device, does not attach any information to the review, and does not receive a copy of what you write.

Anything you post on the App Store or Google Play is submitted to Apple or Google under their own terms and privacy policies, may be publicly visible together with your store nickname, and is stored and moderated by them, not by the Developer. Please do not include personal or sensitive information in a review. The Developer can read public reviews and may reply to them through the store, but has no access to your identity beyond what the store chooses to display.

8. Data retention and deletion

Because the Developer holds no data about you, there is nothing for the Developer to retain or delete. All App content lives on your device and is under your control. You can delete individual notes and reminders inside the App, or delete everything by uninstalling the App. Reviews or comments you post on the App Store or Google Play are retained and can be edited or deleted according to the rules of the respective store. If you contact the Developer directly (for example by email), that correspondence may be kept for as long as needed to handle your request.

9. Children

The App does not knowingly collect any personal data from anyone, including children. Since no data is collected, no parental consent mechanism is required. Purchases are subject to the age and parental-control rules of the App Store and Google Play.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, data-protection laws (such as the GDPR or the CCPA) may give you rights over personal data held about you. Because the Developer does not collect, store, or process any personal data about you, there is no personal data for us to access, correct, port, restrict, or erase. You can exercise full control over your content directly on your device.

11. Security

The App relies on the security features of your device and operating system. The Developer makes no guarantee that the App or your device is free from vulnerabilities and accepts no liability for unauthorized access to, or loss of, content stored on your device.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if a future version of the App adds new features. The current version is always bundled inside the App under Settings → About → Privacy Policy, together with its effective date. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the updated policy.

13. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach the Developer through the support contact details published on the App's listing in the Apple App Store or Google Play. The Settings → About → Report a problem action inside the App takes you to that listing.
